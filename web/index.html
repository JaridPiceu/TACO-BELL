Tensor Archive of Conformal Output — Best Ever Lattice Labour

Each run here comes from a Tensor Network Renormalization (TNR) calculation, computed with TNRKit.jl, on the 2D φ⁴ lattice model. Starting from a bare mass μ₀² and coupling λ, TNR coarse-grains the lattice step by step until the tensor settles onto a fixed point — and the spectrum of that fixed point is a conformal field theory (CFT): a central charge c and a tower of scaling dimensions Δ, organized by symmetry sector.
